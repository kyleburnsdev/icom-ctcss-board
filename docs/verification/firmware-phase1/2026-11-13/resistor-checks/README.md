# Loose-resistor meter records

These original JSON records were retained from the November 13 bench session and
promoted from scratch storage while preparing the learning guide. No new meter
measurements were made during documentation.

`measurement.json` retains the first resistor's initial mode-change readings,
including transient zeros. `settled-repeat.json` is the accepted settled repeat.
`resistor-2.json` through `resistor-5.json` retain the next four parts;
`status.json` retains instrument state. Means identify nominal values of
10 kΩ, 10 kΩ, 10 kΩ, 1 kΩ and 100 Ω, respectively. The learning guide explains
settling, function/range/math checks and the distinction between repeated polls
and independent conversions. These are loose-component readings, not measurements
of installed circuit resistance.
