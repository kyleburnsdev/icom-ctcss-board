# W1.2 final-product wiring guide verification

2026-11-16. Scope: documentation and illustrations for the current saved board;
no circuit, PCB, enclosure, firmware or manufacturing design was changed.

- [Guide](../../final-product-wiring-guide.md) and [32-row wire list](../../reference/final-product-wire-list.csv):
  all 31 header positions, including empty J3-4, plus the local EC11 C-to-S2 jumper.
  Thirty female PCB contacts are populated across six harnesses.
- [Header readback](header-readback.json): fresh Konnect `get_component_pads`
  results for J1–J6, each explicitly `source=file`, bound to
  `electronics/icom-ctcss-board.kicad_pcb`. No live-board edits were used.
- [Schematic netlist](schematic.net): fresh Eeschema 10.0.0 export through
  Konnect, from the current saved schematic. This is an exported evidence file,
  not an editable KiCad source.
- [Checks and SHA-256 identities](checks.json): all header nets compared across
  CSV, PCB readback and schematic export; MIC/RADIO pass-through, intercepted PTT,
  power-rail separation and LED resistor connections checked. Guide local links,
  anchors, row counts and table coverage checked.
- Seven figures generated by [render-wiring-guide.py](../../../tools/render-wiring-guide.py)
  from the saved pad coordinates and wire schedule, with schematic device views
  explicitly distinguished from exact connector/encoder terminal geometry.
  All seven images were visually inspected; the plug-view caption and shield
  label spacing were corrected before the final inspection.
  W1.1 adds front/mating and rear/solder GX16-8 views for both sexes, with
  seven perimeter contacts and center contact 8; rear views are geometric
  left/right mirrors of their matching front views. Numbering follows
  [Renhotec's male/female diagrams](https://renhotecpro.com/gx16-connector-data-sheet/).
  It is a reference numbering layout, not a measured mapping through the owned
  adapters. The EC11-style front/rear illustration shows the 3+2 terminal groups,
  mechanical ears, arbitrary A/B and S1/S2 assignment, and local ground jumper.
  Geometry references the [PEC11R-4 drawing, page 2](https://www.bourns.com/docs/product-datasheets/pec11r.pdf);
  its exact terminal identities are not attributed to the generic WWZMDiB part.
- W1.2 adds two perspective hardware illustrations from
  [render-wiring-hardware.py](../../../tools/render-wiring-hardware.py), for nine
  figures total. These show the physical GX16 shell, threads, nut, locating key,
  male pins, female socket openings and male rear solder cups; the EC11 shaft,
  mounting ears and opposing 3/2 solder-tab groups are visible in shaft-side
  and underside views. The 3/2 arrangement and absent markings are established
  user inputs. Center common is a conventional EC11 expectation to confirm by
  the described meter check, not a measured result. Views are illustrative
  geometry, not manufacturing CAD or photographs. Both new figures were visually
  inspected for contact visibility, callout placement and body/terminal identity.

Rebuild the figures/tables with `python tools/render-wiring-guide.py` (Python
3.10+, Pillow, Windows Arial fonts). Run `python tools/check-wiring-guide.py`
to check the retained snapshot. After an electrical revision, refresh Konnect
header readback and export the schematic netlist before checking the new
schedule; use `--netlist PATH` to incorporate that fresh exported evidence.
Source hashes in `checks.json` identify which board/schematic accompanied W1;
the retained snapshot is not an automatic assertion about later PCB edits.

Manufacturer references consulted on 2026-11-16: Seeed's
[LCD page](https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/Grove-LCD_RGB_Backlight/) and
[Grove pin convention](https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/Grove_System/), Harwin's
[M20-1160042 compatibility](https://www.harwin.com/products/M20-1160042) and
[IS-15 issue 8 crimp instructions](https://www.harwin.com/api/download/?document=https://content.harwin.com/m/7d24feda1954c6ac/original/IS-15-IS-15-Z20-320-Hand-Crimp-Tool-Instruction-Sheet.pdf).
Harwin values in the guide are conditional references, not an identification of
the user's owned generic contacts. Wire gauge, colors, solder starting settings
and continuity/isolation limits are selected workshop recommendations.

Inventory discovery used the authoritative workshop store and selected EC11,
LED and stripper/crimper records. The records do not establish flexible-wire
stock or contact-specific crimp-tool compatibility. This documentation task
does not select a new physical BOM or machine operation; no fabrication
preflight pass, stock reservation or physical qualification is claimed.

**Physical acceptance remains separate:** the owned adapter cavity maps, generic
EC11 terminal roles/direction, final route lengths, crimp retention, assembled
power/thermal margin, actual radio load/PTT/deviation and enclosure clearances
must be recorded when the harnesses are built. The guide contains the procedures
and worksheets for those checks. The prior Grove/fixture results retain their
original scope and are not claimed as a test of these new final harnesses.
